## FAQ — 5 Real Questions

### Q: Is this actually free?
**A:** Yes, completely free. CostPilot is built by Credex, which sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. If your audit shows significant savings, we'll surface Credex as a way to capture even more. If not, you still keep your audit. No catch, no spam.

### Q: How accurate is the pricing data?
**A:** Every price is sourced directly from official vendor pricing pages and verified at submission time. We cite every source in PRICING_DATA.md. If a price is wrong, email us and we'll fix it within 24 hours.

### Q: Do I need to create an account?
**A:** No. You complete the full audit without signing up. We only ask for your email after showing you the results — and only if you want to save or share the report.

### Q: What data do you collect?
**A:** We store the tools, plans, and spend amounts you enter to generate your audit. If you provide your email, we store that too. We never sell your data. Shareable URLs strip out all PII (email, company name, role).

### Q: How is this different from just checking prices myself?
**A:** Three things: (1) cross-vendor alternative analysis for your specific use case, (2) plan right-sizing based on your actual team size, and (3) Credex credit pricing — which can save 15–30% on top of plan optimization alone. Doing this manually takes hours; CostPilot does it in 2 minutes.
