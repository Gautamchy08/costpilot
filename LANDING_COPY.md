## FAQ — 5 Real Questions

### Q: Is this actually free?
**A:** Yes, completely free. CostPilot is built by Credex, which sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. If your audit shows significant savings, we'll offer to help you capture those savings through Credex. If not, you still keep the audit. No catch.

### Q: How accurate is the pricing data?
**A:** Every price in our engine is sourced from official vendor pricing pages and verified weekly. We cite every source in our documentation. If a price is wrong, email us and we'll fix it within 24 hours.

### Q: Do I need to create an account?
**A:** No. You can complete the full audit without signing up. We only ask for your email after showing you the results — and only if you want to save the report or get notified about new savings opportunities.

### Q: What data do you collect?
**A:** We store the tools, plans, and spend numbers you enter (to generate your audit). If you provide your email, we store that too. We never sell your data. Shareable report URLs strip out any identifying information (email, company name).

### Q: How is this different from just checking prices myself?
**A:** You could compare prices manually, but CostPilot does three things you can't easily do yourself: (1) cross-vendor alternative analysis for your specific use case, (2) plan right-sizing based on your team size, and (3) benchmark your per-developer AI spend against similar companies. Plus, we check Credex credit availability, which can save an additional 15–30% on top of plan optimization.
