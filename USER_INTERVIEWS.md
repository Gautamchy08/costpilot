> ⚠️ **IMPORTANT REMINDER:** These interviews MUST be with real humans. Fabricated interviews are an instant reject. Talk to 3 people this week — cold DM founders on X, ask in indie hacker Slacks, use your college network, talk to friends running side projects or working at startups.
>
> **Suggested questions to ask:**
> 1. What AI tools does your team pay for? How much roughly per month?
> 2. Have you ever audited whether you're on the right plan?
> 3. What would make you switch AI tools? Price? Features? Both?
> 4. If a free tool showed you exactly where you're overspending, would you use it?
> 5. Would you share your audit results with your manager/CFO?
